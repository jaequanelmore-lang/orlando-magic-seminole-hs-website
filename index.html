Orlando Magic

About the Magic

The Orlando Magic is a professional basketball team based in Orlando, Florida. Playing in the Eastern Conference of the NBA, the Magic has a rich history of excellence and passionate fan support.

Current Roster

The Magic features some of the most talented players in the NBA, competing at the highest level of professional basketball with determination and skill.

Amway Center

Home to the Orlando Magic, the Amway Center is a state-of-the-art arena that hosts thrilling games and world-class entertainment throughout the season.

Seminole High School

School Spirit

Seminole High School is home to the Seminoles, a proud athletic program with a strong tradition of excellence, academic achievement, and community involvement.

Athletic Programs

From basketball to football, track to soccer, Seminole High School offers diverse athletic opportunities for students to develop their skills and compete at high levels.

Community

The Seminole community represents school pride, teamwork, and dedication. Students, athletes, and families come together to support their teams and celebrate achievements.
